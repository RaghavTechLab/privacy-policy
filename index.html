Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Thank you for using Gallery App. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles user information. We have designed this app to be a private, offline-first utility.

1. Information Collection and Use

Gallery App is a simple tool for viewing and managing media[cite: 1]. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information[cite: 1]. No data is transmitted from your device to any external servers[cite: 1].

2. Device Permissions

The app only requests permissions necessary for core gallery functionality:

  • Media Storage (Read & Write): Required to display your images/videos and organize them into albums.
  • Delete Permission: Used only when you explicitly choose to delete a file within the app[cite: 1].

All processing, such as loading thumbnails or moving files, happens entirely on your local hardware[cite: 1].

3. No Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics tools like Firebase. Your usage habits and files remain completely private and are never shared with third parties[cite: 1].

4. Data Security

Because we do not use internet-based services or cloud storage, your data remains under your physical control on your device[cite: 1, 2].
